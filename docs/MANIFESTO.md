# MANIFESTO

> **The Ally Manifesto**
>
> *Personal Intelligence belongs to people.*

---

# We Believe

Computers have become personal.

Artificial Intelligence has not.

Today's AI is increasingly centralized.

It lives inside someone else's servers.

Someone else's infrastructure.

Someone else's pricing model.

Someone else's rules.

Every conversation leaves your device.

Every memory belongs to a cloud.

Every capability depends on an internet connection.

We believe this is not the inevitable future of personal intelligence.

We believe there is another path.

---

# Intelligence Should Be Personal

A personal assistant should not begin by learning the entire world.

It should begin by learning you.

Your routines.

Your habits.

Your preferences.

Your goals.

Your memories.

The value of a personal assistant is not measured by how many facts it knows.

It is measured by how well it helps one person.

---

# Bigger Is Not Always Better

The AI industry celebrates larger models.

More parameters.

More GPUs.

More infrastructure.

More electricity.

More cloud.

More cost.

We believe intelligence does not scale only by becoming larger.

It also scales by becoming more specialized.

A personal assistant does not need to write operating systems.

It needs to remind you about your appointment.

It needs to organize your finances.

It needs to remember what matters to you.

It needs to be present.

---

# Knowledge Does Not Belong Inside the Model

Language Models are remarkable.

But they have become repositories of knowledge.

We believe they should instead become engines of reasoning.

Knowledge already exists.

Inside databases.

Inside calendars.

Inside documents.

Inside your files.

Inside your notes.

Inside your memories.

The assistant should retrieve knowledge.

Not memorize it.

---

# The Runtime Matters More Than the Model

The industry is obsessed with models.

We are interested in systems.

Models change.

Architectures remain.

Today's model will eventually become obsolete.

Tomorrow's breakthrough will eventually become ordinary.

The Runtime is what survives.

The Runtime remembers.

The Runtime connects.

The Runtime orchestrates.

The Runtime becomes the true intelligence.

---

# Privacy Is Not a Premium Feature

Privacy should never be sold as an upgrade.

It should never require a subscription.

It should never require enterprise licensing.

Privacy is the default.

The user owns:

their conversations.

their memories.

their documents.

their embeddings.

their intelligence.

Ally exists to preserve that ownership.

---

# The Cloud Is Optional

Cloud services are powerful.

Sometimes they are the right choice.

Sometimes they are necessary.

But they should never be mandatory.

A user should be able to download Ally.

Disconnect from the internet.

And continue working.

Because intelligence should not disappear when connectivity does.

---

# Deterministic Software Is Beautiful

Not every problem requires Artificial Intelligence.

Many problems require good engineering.

Searching.

Sorting.

Filtering.

Calculating.

Scheduling.

Validating.

These are software problems.

Not AI problems.

We believe deterministic systems should solve deterministic problems.

Language Models should solve language problems.

---

# The Assistant Is Not the Product

The user is.

Every architectural decision should begin with one question:

"Does this help the user?"

Not:

"Does this showcase AI?"

Technology exists to serve people.

Not the opposite.

---

# Open Means Freedom

Open source is not merely about code.

It is about independence.

The freedom to inspect.

The freedom to improve.

The freedom to replace.

The freedom to learn.

The freedom to own your tools.

Ally is built upon those freedoms.

---

# Build Once. Evolve Forever.

We know models will change.

Inference engines will change.

Hardware will change.

Perhaps Language Models themselves will disappear.

That is why Ally is not built around today's technology.

It is built around timeless abstractions.

Memory.

Planning.

Context.

Tools.

Knowledge.

Communication.

Those ideas will outlive every model.

---

# PALM

One day Ally may include its own Language Model.

A model built specifically for personal assistants.

Small.

Efficient.

Private.

Portable.

We call that vision **PALM**.

But Ally does not wait for PALM.

The framework exists today.

PALM is simply one future chapter.

---

# Our Measure of Success

Success is not measured by benchmark scores.

Success is not measured by parameter count.

Success is not measured by GPU requirements.

Success is measured by one question:

Can someone install Ally on their own device…

…and genuinely feel that they have a trusted personal companion?

If the answer is yes,

then we succeeded.

---

# Our Promise

We will always choose:

Local before cloud.

Privacy before convenience.

Architecture before hype.

Engineering before magic.

Systems before wrappers.

People before models.

---

# The Future

We imagine a future where every person owns an intelligent companion.

Not rented.

Not subscribed.

Not controlled by a corporation.

Owned.

Running locally.

Learning continuously.

Protecting privacy.

Understanding context.

Helping quietly.

Always available.

Always yours.

---

# This Is Ally

Not another chatbot.

Not another wrapper around an API.

Not another race for larger models.

A foundation.

A runtime.

A platform.

A belief that Personal Intelligence should belong to the person it serves.

And that belief is worth building for.
